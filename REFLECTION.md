## What I asked Copilot to build
I asked Copilot to build a website that takes my school calendar and creates a CSV file that I can import into Google Calendar. At first, I broke the problem down into three files: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I tried to build each file with AI separately.

## My approach to questions
I started with very broad questions to get my HTML layout correct before asking the AI to style it with my school colors and add buttons. After settling the broad style questions, I got more specific with my implementation. I eventually shared what one of my school calendars looked like so the AI could implement my feature properly.

## What surprised me in development
What surprised me was how quickly it generated code and how much code it produced. I was also surprised that, when you get really specific, it gives you exactly what you need.

## What I learned
I learned that AI has access to a lot of online tools. At one point during my implementation, I asked it to use AI in the conversion process. It had a clear approach, but I scrapped it because it was too confusing for me to understand.

## What I would do differently
I would be more precise and use simpler prompts at the start. I think I tried to cover too many things with my first couple of prompts. Not Use AI for README.
